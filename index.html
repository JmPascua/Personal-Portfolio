01 · Profile

About.log

I've always enjoyed figuring out how things work, whether that's a car, a motorcycle, or a piece of software.

My interest in technology has grown alongside my interest in working with things hands-on. Outside of school and work, I've spent a lot of time around cars and motorcycles, learning how they work, working on them, and figuring out what went wrong and how to fix it. That same curiosity eventually carried over into my study of Computer Science.

During my time at Northern Illinois University, I worked on projects involving databases, web applications, Android development, and general software development. I worked with classmates on group projects, including an inventory management system for automotive parts and a karaoke management system. Outside of those projects, I've continued experimenting on my own, including developing this portfolio while learning new technologies and improving the ones I already know. I also branched out into the niche world of enterprise computing through IBM Z, earning several certifications and gaining hands-on experience with mainframe systems.

Outside of academics, I've spent the last two years working at Discount Tire as a Service Technician. I've worked on a wide range of vehicles, from electric to gas-powered, while using specialized equipment to inspect, diagnose, and service them. I've also worked directly with customers, assisted with sales, and used inventory systems to track products and orders.

I'm still continuing to develop, experiment, learn, and grow professionally.
